Incident Clock Demo

NIS2, GDPR, MDR and CRA deadlines for one incident.

More

This replaces the current incident and its decision log.

The current incident is kept under Earlier incidents in Close.

This is an example incident with made-up details. Choose More, then New incident, to start a blank one.

  1. The clock starts at the time you became aware of the incident. You can change everything later.

    All deadlines count from this time.

  2. Answer what you know. Unsure keeps the clock running, so you do not miss a deadline while you find out.

  3. Which laws apply

    • When did incident handling end?

      This sets the NIS2 final report date. Leave it empty while you are still handling the incident.

      When was a fix or mitigation available?

      This starts the CRA final report clock: 14 days, to the end of the day.
